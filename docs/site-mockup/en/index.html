How to play

Three steps, every round.

  1. 1

    Bid

    Everyone is dealt the same number of cards and says how many tricks they will take. The last bidder may not make the bids add up to the tricks, so somebody is always wrong.

  2. 2

    Play

    Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play a trump if you have one. The highest trump wins, or else the highest card of the suit led.

  3. 3

    Score

    Take exactly what you bid: 5 points plus your bid. Miss, and you lose the difference. Five exact bids in a row earn 10 more.
