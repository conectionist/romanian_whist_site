About the game

Say how many tricks you will take. Then take exactly that many.

The game

Romanian Whist is a trick-taking card game for 2 to 6 players. Every round, each player bids how many tricks they will take, and the whole point is to be exactly right.

A game runs 1-8-1, one card up to eight and back down, or 8-1-8, the other way round. In a forehead round you see everyone's card but your own. In a hidden round nobody sees anything before bidding.

The rules are enforced for you: cards you may not play are greyed out, and the one bid the last bidder may not make is unavailable.

Rules and scoring

1

The deal

Every player is dealt the same number of cards, and the next card off the deck is turned up as trump. Rounds of eight cards have no trump.

2

The bidding

Everyone bids how many tricks they will take. The last bidder may not make the bids add up to the number of tricks, so somebody is always going to be wrong.

3

The play

Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, you must play a trump if you have one. The highest trump wins the trick, or the highest card of the suit led if no trump was played.

At the end of a round
You took exactly what you bid5 + your bid
You missedminus the difference
5th exact bid in a row+10 bonus
5th miss in a row−10

One-card rounds don't count towards streaks. The highest total at the end of the game wins.

Every detail, including the round schedules, is in the engine's rules document.

Nine computer opponents

Each seat can play a different one. Pick a gentle table, or one that counts every card.

Opponent How it plays
RandomBids a random legal number and plays a random legal card.
First cardBids 0 (or 1 when 0 is barred) and plays the first legal card in its hand.
Low riskBids the tricks its hand will take anyway, then wins them cheaply.
DuckingBids 0 no matter what, and gets rid of its high cards whenever they can't win.
CyborgWorks out exact odds for every card, bids the count with the best expected score, and re-plans every trick.
Reckoner AlphaImagines no deals and plays by rule, but sometimes plays a random card.
Reckoner Beta, Gamma, DeltaImagine possible deals before each card and play what does best across them. Beta sometimes plays at random; Gamma and Delta never do.

Open source

One C++20 engine holds every rule and every computer opponent. Each version of the game is a client of it, so they all play exactly the same game.

  • EngineAGPL-3.0 The rules, the scoring and the computer opponents. No user interface. romanian_whist_engine
  • DesktopGPL-3.0 The desktop app for Windows, Linux and macOS. romanian_whist_desktop
  • TerminalGPL-3.0 The game in a terminal, played from the keyboard. romanian_whist_terminal
  • ServerAGPL-3.0 Runs the online tables and solo games for the browser and Android. romanian_whist_server
  • BrowserAGPL-3.0 The page you play online in, in the desktop app's look. romanian_whist_web
  • AndroidGPL-3.0 · planned The phone app. The plan and the mock-up are there; the app is next. romanian_whist_android
